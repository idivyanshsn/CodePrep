## Project Overview

**CodePrep** is a full-stack AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to provide candidates with a realistic, personalized interview experience.

Instead of practicing generic interview questions, CodePrep analyzes a candidate's:

- Resume
- Target role
- Experience level
- Technical skills
- Projects
- Interview type

and uses this information to generate contextual interview questions.

The platform also supports voice-based interviews, answer submission, interview history, performance analytics, and downloadable interview reports.

### Why CodePrep?

Traditional interview preparation platforms often provide static question banks.

CodePrep follows a more personalized approach:

```text
Candidate Resume
       ↓
Resume Parsing & Analysis
       ↓
Candidate Profile
       ↓
AI Question Generation
       ↓
Technical / Voice Interview
       ↓
Answer Evaluation
       ↓
Performance Analytics
       ↓
Interview Report
